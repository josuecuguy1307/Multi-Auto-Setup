BROKE:
1. USB D- ESD protection is bypassed. PA11 (U1.32) reaches J1 A7/B7 by a direct copper path of 19.9-21.0 mm that never touches U2 (path leaves the A7-B7 bridge at (10.7,8.1) and passes right of U2 via (11.5,8.9)-(13.55,11.35)-(15.65,19.35)). U2's D- pins 3/4 hang on a separate 15.2 mm stub (B7 -> (10.75,6.85) -> (8.4,6.05) -> (5.1,5.45) -> (4.85,7.95) -> (8.05,11.1) -> U2.3), under the USB-C body and out past its shell tab. D+ goes through U2 correctly (J1 -> U2.1 -> U2.6 -> U1.33). A-1 §6 item 2 says "ESD part at the connector" and A-3 says J1 -> U2 within 3 mm, D+/D- "short, paired". This is not disclosed: HUMAN_REVIEW only says "not length-matched" and lists D+, but not D-, under the USB-C body. D- is 39.4 mm of copper; D+ is 23.8 mm.
2. Vias inside SMD pads, against A-3 ("a via beside each GND pad"), and not disclosed. 31 of the 71 vias have their centre inside an SMD pad. The drill file confirms open 0.3 mm plated holes, for example X103.0Y-74.2 in C1's GND pad. Affected: the GND pad of every 0603 passive (C1-C12, R1-R3), U2.2 GND, U2.5 VBUS, U3.2 GND, SW1 pads, J3 pads 3/4/5/9, D1.1, R4.2, R5.1, R6.2, C3.1 and C10.1. The +3V3 via at (14.6,21.7) has its drill hole overlapping U1 pin 36, a 0.3 mm-wide pad at 0.5 mm pitch on the LQFP. With reflow, solder wicks into these holes, which risks starved joints and tombstoning. KiCad DRC does not check this, so "fabricability green" does not cover it.
