# A sensor

A number without a unit is not a measurement. Your job is to find out what is measured and how far to trust it.

## find
See how it is attached: a serial port, a bus, a Bluetooth service. The channel limits what you may try.

## read
Take several readings, not one. Range, rhythm and noise say more than a single value. Volatile facts such as a live temperature are recorded as volatile and never judged wrong for changing.

## compare
Two independent sources that agree make a fact verified; two that disagree make it contradicted, and both stay on record.

## ask
The unit and the quantity are often only in the manual or in the owner's head. Ask once, with the fact key.
